When moonshot released Kimi K3.1 pro, they actively stated it competes with Opus 5.5. Though when there was testing done to kimi k3.1 pro its behavior was extremely suspicious, it seemed to be heavily trained on benchmarks. The research paper link I’m about to talk about is included in the photo, it shows Kimis behavior. it was conducted by @JSchaeff3r on Twitter. This paper showed that Kimi k3.1 pro would actively not follow any slightly manipulated instructions in a prompt and actively ignore them, leading it to failing tasks outside of benchmarks. In its reasoning, Kimi often tries to figure out which benchmark it’s being tested for and it would recall its training and certain details that were supposed to help it do the task even though those details were not in a prompt. Benchmaxxing is when an AI is trained on benchmarks while lacking actual ability to do tasks in the real world, it seems apparent that kimis training was rough to the point where it actively ignores slightly changed instructions and follows whatever it remembers in its training without actually reasoning.
